PressureFitting Class

The drawing shape used to represent items such as tees, wyes, elbows and reducers used in utility networks. A fitting connects pipes together to form a pipe network.

Remarks
PressureFittings represent physical objects such as tees, wyes, elbows and reducers. A PressureFitting is created by using the AddFitting () method in a PressurePipeNetwork object.
