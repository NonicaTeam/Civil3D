A GeneralSurfaceProperties object that contains information about the surface.
Exceptions
ExceptionCondition
InvalidOperationException Thrown when surface object has no triangle or cell in it.
Remarks
The general properties of a surface include information about minimum and maximum x and y coordinates, elevation, and number of points.
