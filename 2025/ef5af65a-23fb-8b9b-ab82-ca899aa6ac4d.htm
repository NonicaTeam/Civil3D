AlignmentDistanceToAlignment Method

Overload List
 NameDescription
DistanceToAlignment(Double, Alignment, Double, Double) Computes the distance to another Alignment. If the target Alignment crosses the current Alignment, this method checks the distance to the target Alignment on both sides, and returns the shorter distance.
DistanceToAlignment(Double, Alignment, AlignmentSide, Double, Double) Computes the distance to another Alignment, specifying the side of current Alignment to look for the target Alignment.
